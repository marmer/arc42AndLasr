What is LASR
(Lightweight Approach for Software Reviews)

  • LASR — Lightweight Approach for Software Reviews
    • By Stefan Zörner and Stefan Toth
  • Again, concentrated knowledge — extracts from various review methods
  • Extremely lightweight
    • Can be stopped/aborted at any time
    • Few participants needed
    • Works internally within teams as well as with external reviewers
  • Delivers fast, management-ready results
  • Gamification!
    • Works wonderfully online and offline
  • Fits into agile processes
    • Assign steps to sprints
    • Plan 1–2h per sprint
